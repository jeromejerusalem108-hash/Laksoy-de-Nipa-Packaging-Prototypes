Participant Information

Participant Information and Consent

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate in this research. You may ask the research team to clarify anything you do not understand.

1. About the Research

Study title: Development and Preliminary Evaluation of Laksoy Brand Identity and Packaging Designs

PROJECT L.A.K.A.S. is a research initiative that focuses on developing and preliminarily evaluating proposed brand identity and packaging designs for Laksoy.

The study aims to gather feedback about the visual presentation, cultural representation, clarity, and overall design of the proposed concepts.

2. Who May Participate?

Eligibility requirement: Only individuals who are at least 21 years old may participate in this evaluation.

Individuals below 21 years old are not eligible to participate and must not proceed to the evaluation.

3. What Will Participants Do?

Participants will be invited to examine five proposed Laksoy packaging design presentations and provide feedback through the F1 evaluation questionnaire.

The evaluation may involve:

  • Viewing the proposed design presentations.
  • Assessing the visible design elements.
  • Answering the evaluation statements.
  • Providing comments about strengths and possible improvements.

The activity focuses on design impressions and observable design features.

Important: No alcohol tasting or consumption is involved. Participation does not require purchasing, selling, or buying any product.

4. Voluntary Participation and Your Rights

Participation in this research is entirely voluntary. You may:

  • Choose whether or not to participate.
  • Skip a question you do not wish to answer.
  • Select "Cannot judge or not assessable" when appropriate.
  • Stop participating without giving a reason.

Your decision will not affect your grades, services, or relationship with the researchers or school.

If you wish to request the removal of your responses, you may do so before the withdrawal deadline specified in the approved consent document.

5. Privacy and Handling of Responses

Research responses will use respondent codes rather than names or learner reference numbers.

The adult coordinator will keep consent records and any identity key separately from the evaluation responses. The student researchers will analyze coded responses and present group summaries.

Identifiable photographs or quotations will require separate permission. Permission to use quotations will not be assumed.
