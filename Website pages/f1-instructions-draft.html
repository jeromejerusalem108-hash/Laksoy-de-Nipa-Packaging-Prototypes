PROJECT L.A.K.A.S.

F1 – Packaging Design Evaluation

Before You Begin

Welcome to the digital evaluation portion of PROJECT L.A.K.A.S. Please read the following instructions carefully before evaluating the packaging design sketches.

1. Overview of the Evaluation

In this evaluation, you will examine five packaging design sketches, identified as S1, S2, S3, S4, and S5.

  • Each design will be presented separately.
  • Each design contains 12 evaluation statements.
  • You will select a response for every evaluation statement.
  • You will also be provided with two sections for written comments about each design.

2. Understanding the Rating Scale

Please use the following scale when evaluating each statement.

5
Strongly Agree
4
Agree
3
Neutral
2
Disagree
1
Strongly Disagree
CJ
Cannot Judge
Reminder:

Select "Cannot Judge" if you feel that you do not have enough information or cannot reasonably assess a particular statement.

3. How to Evaluate Each Design

  1. Examine the displayed packaging design carefully before answering.
  2. Read each evaluation statement and select the response that best represents your assessment.
  3. Answer all 12 statements for the respective design.
  4. Use the comment sections to provide additional observations, suggestions, or recommendations.
  5. Review your answers before proceeding to the next design.

4. Important Reminders

  • There are five designs to evaluate.
  • Provide honest answers based on your own observations.
  • There are no right or wrong opinions in this evaluation.
  • Take your time when examining each packaging design.
  • Ensure that your responses are complete before submitting.
About F2:

The F2 physical prototype evaluation will be conducted separately using a pen-and-paper questionnaire. It is not part of this digital evaluation process.

5. Ready to Begin?

Once you have understood the instructions, you may proceed to the F1 packaging design evaluation.

Thank you for participating in PROJECT L.A.K.A.S.
